# Adidas-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Adidas sales, profit, products, retailers, regions, and monthly sales trends using DAX, Power Query, KPIs, and interactive filters.
Dataset link : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heemalichaudhari/adidas-sales-dataset

DASHBOARD VISUALIZATION REQUIREMENTS

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

• Monthly Sales Trend – Analyzes sales performance across different months.
• Sales by State – Visualizes the geographical distribution of sales.
• Sales by Region – Compares revenue contribution across different regions.
• Sales by Product – Identifies the best-selling product categories.
• Sales by Retailer – Compares retailer performance based on total sales.
• Sales Method Slicer – Filters the report based on sales method.
• Date Slicer – Filters the report by month and year.

These visualizations help stakeholders understand sales trends, regional performance, customer channels, and product demand, enabling informed business decisions.


KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

The dashboard provides the following KPIs:

1. Total Sales – Overall revenue generated.
2. Total Profit – Total profit earned from sales.
3. Total Units Sold – Number of units sold.
4. Average Price per Unit – Average selling price of products.
5. Average Profit Margin (%) – Percentage of profit earned on sales.

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the company's overall business performance.
